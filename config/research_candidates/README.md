# Research Candidates — README

These files document the signal-feasibility and threshold-calibration study
performed on REAL two-year history (2024-09-23 .. 2026-09-23 UTC) for
XAUUSD / GBPJPY / EURUSD on M15 / M30 / H1.

## Status

Every candidate in this directory is **STUDIED and NOT PROMOTED**. Nothing
here is production configuration. Production defaults live only in
`config/strategy_params.py` and are unchanged by this study.

## How to read the files

- `candidates_M15.yaml`, `candidates_M30.yaml`, `candidates_H1.yaml`
  list the drop thresholds, volume screens, ATR-multiple thresholds, dynamic
  trailing-percentile windows and floors that were tested on each timeframe,
  the observed signal counts, the relaxed full-detector-stack counts, and the
  realised research-simulation result for the most active configs.

## Why nothing was promoted

1. The production detector stack (drop 3% AND volume 2x AND ATR-spike 1.5x
   AND a same-bar reversal pattern) fires **zero** times over two years of real
   data on every symbol and timeframe. Relaxing the thresholds does not help:
   reversal candlestick patterns are structurally incompatible with large
   drop bars (~1% of large-drop bars carry a pattern vs ~27% of all bars).
2. Even dropping the pattern + ATR-spike conditions and trading only
   drop + volume with production trade management, every candidate loses
   money (PF 0.68..0.99, negative returns over two years) and shows no
   forward-return edge over a random baseline.
3. GBPJPY minimum-lot sizing refuses 100% of signals at every tested account
   size (0.01 lot needs ~127k at 2% risk / 1.5% stop with a 100000 contract).
