The Question

Why do most automated trading systems fail to achieve sustainable results?

The Original Idea

Wait for a market "Bad Luck Moment" — a capitulation — then enter at the moment of maximum apparent loss probability, because that is precisely when reversal probability peaks. Protect the idea with "organized randomness" so institutional algorithms can't predict it.

The Real Question

Does any systematically designed, rigorously tested, statistically honest trading strategy produce a real, out-of-sample edge on actual market data? Not "a pretty equity curve." Not "a backtest that looks amazing." Real edge, or nothing.

Key Findings

Seven discoveries, all backed by reproducible numbers.

FINDING 01

The Premise is Dead

0 signals / 2y
A 3% M15 drop never truly happens. Largest on record: 3.6% (once, and it failed the volume gate). The "Bad Luck Moment" as defined does not exist.
FINDING 02

Relaxed Thresholds Have No Edge

PF 0.07–0.99
983 signals on XAUUSD M15 with relaxed gates, but forward returns are indistinguishable from random. Every realised simulation loses money.
FINDING 03

GBPJPY is Untradeable

~$127k / 0.01 lot
Minimum-lot sizing requires ~$127k at 2% risk. 100% signal refusal even at $100k accounts. A structural barrier, not a parameter issue.
FINDING 04

EURUSD is Perfectly Efficient

0 / 2000 survive
Not one random strategy survived even the first filter. The strongest evidence in this study that liquid FX is informationally efficient at M15.
FINDING 05

Only Directional Beta Survives

SHORT −12.86%
Both survivors are LONG-only on a rising asset. The SHORT mirror loses money; buy-and-hold gold (+62.9%) beats the strategy 6x. Surviving is not the same as being good.
FINDING 06

The 65% Win-Rate Illusion

skew −2.16
A tight +0.5% TP clips 85 wins at a fixed +0.47%; 86 losers extend to max duration. Fat left tail, kurtosis +7.4. The win rate is a presentation artifact, not an edge.

Alpha vs Beta

What we hoped we had found — and what forensic analysis actually showed.

What we hoped

The Alpha Story

A statistically filtered strategy with a 65.2% win rate, profit factor 1.73, Sharpe 1.26, and a p(luck) below 3%. The mining funnel said it beat random by a mile.

It sat at the 99.8th percentile of 500 random LONG strategies.

What it actually was

The Beta Truth

SHORT mirror: −12.86%. Buy-and-hold gold: +62.93% vs strategy +10.91%. Profits live only in strong uptrends; the single losing quarter and the negative OOS window both align with gold declines.

The strategy is a noisy, low-leverage way of being long gold. Real alpha: zero.

Lessons Learned

The market is more efficient than we imagined. A 4,000-strategy search across two liquid instruments found zero exploitable intraday M15 alpha.

Most "strategies" sold commercially are disguised beta. Ours was. We only found out because we ran the SHORT mirror and buy-and-hold controls.

A 65% win rate can be a presentation artifact. Winners clipped tiny, losers allowed to bleed, symmetric risk hiding asymmetric reward.

Random mining plus statistical correction is the only honest method. Measure chance, control the false discovery rate, then autopsy the survivors.

A truthful NO-GO is worth more than a deceptive YES. It saved us — and any user — from deploying disguised beta at scale into a regime change.

The engine is the real achievement. It refused to lie to us, and it can test any future hypothesis honestly.
