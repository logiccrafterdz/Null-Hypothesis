The Bottom Line
B — DIRECTIONAL BETA, NOT ALPHA

The survivor strategies were not market-neutral skill. They were LONG-only bets on a rising asset. When reversed, the identical strategy lost money. A passive "buy and hold" made 6× more than the best mined strategy. Every statistical filter was passed — and the forensic autopsy still detected no alpha. The null hypothesis was not rejected.

Scope: XAUUSD & EURUSD · 2 years of real M15 data · the detectors, filters, mining rules and constraints we built. This is a failure to reject the null — not a proof that markets are efficient everywhere or always.

The Question

Why Do Trading Systems Fail?

Thousands of "proven" systems flood retail markets every year — almost all of them curve-fitted backtests or disguised exposure. This project asked one question with scientific discipline: does a rigorously tested, statistically honest strategy produce a real, out-of-sample edge on real market data?

🕯️ The Original Idea

A "Bad Luck Moment" strategy: wait for the market to capitulate (big drop + volume + volatility + reversal pattern), then enter at the moment of maximum apparent loss probability — because that is precisely when reversal probability peaks. Protect the edge with "organized randomness" (a cryptographic random decision engine) so institutional algorithms can't predict entries.

🔬 The Real Question

Not "can we draw a pretty equity curve?" — anyone can. The real question: after curvier filters, luck baselines, false-discovery-rate correction, out-of-sample walk-forwards and a full forensic autopsy, is there any reproducible edge that is not just leverage into a bull market?

Phase 7 · Reality Check

The Original Premise is Dead

❌ 0 Signals in 2 Years

First contact with real FBS MT5 broker data. The full detector fired exactly zero times in two years on XAUUSD, GBPJPY, EURUSD.

▸ Details

A 3% single-bar M15 drop is a 99.9th-percentile event for these instruments. It happened once in two years — and that one bar failed the volume gate. Combining drop + volume + volatility + reversal pattern made complete signals structurally impossible.

Phase 9 · Pivot

The Strategy Pivot

❌ NO-GO

Restructured the thesis into two new variants. Both were tested rigorously. Both failed. The market kept its secrets.

▸ Details
  • Next-Bar Reversal — the reversal pattern is evaluated on the bar after the capitulation bar, giving a correct no-lookahead structure. Result: no edge.
  • Panic Continuation — instead of fading the panic, ride it. Result: no edge.

Reversal patterns are structurally incompatible with large-drop bars: patterns exist on ~27% of bars but on only ~1% of large-drop bars. This motivated quick, high-frequency no-lookahead variants — neither produced exploitable forward returns over the two-year real corpus.

Phase 10 · Chaos Mining

Random Hypothesis Mining

⚠️ 2 of 4,000 Survived

Abandoned human logic. Generated 4,000 random strategies from a 50+ condition library, applied a statistical gauntlet. XAUUSD yielded two survivors; EURUSD yielded zero.

The mining engine first had to exactly reproduce the Phase-8 reference backtest before it was allowed to mine anything. Then every generated strategy faced: an 800-strategy random-entry luck baseline, a primary filter, robustness stress, and FDR correction (Benjamini-Hochberg) + p95 luck percentile.

Lessons Learned

What the Journey Taught Us

📉
Under our methods, data and constraints, a 4,000-strategy search across two liquid instruments detected zero exploitable intraday M15 alpha. We failed to reject the null hypothesis — we did not "prove" that markets are efficient everywhere.
🎭
Most "strategies" sold commercially are disguised beta. Ours was — and we only found out because we ran the SHORT-mirror and buy-and-hold controls nobody else runs.
🧮
A 65% win rate can be a presentation artifact: winners clipped tiny, losers allowed to bleed, asymmetric risk hiding asymmetric reward.
🎲
Random mining + statistical correction is the only honest method. Measure chance, control the false-discovery rate, then autopsy the survivors.
🚫
A truthful NO-GO is worth more than a deceptive YES. It saved real money that a disguised-beta deployment at scale would have lost.
🔬
The engine is the real achievement. It refused to lie to us — and it can test any future hypothesis honestly.
